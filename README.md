Blocks App — ForAI Music Creators
Nov 15, 2026 · @Bobby
Blocks App is a fast Windows studio for people who make music with AI. It keeps every
song you make in one place on your own computer: all the takes, covers, lyrics, style
prompts and release details. It plays them through a high-end player with live visuals. It
helps you write the next one, and it gets the finished song ready for streaming with one
click.
It's built for Suno creators, and works with any audio file you already have: MP3, WAV,
FLAC, M4A, OGG orAIFF.
Why it exists
AI music creators end up with hundreds of takes spread across downloads folders,
browser tabs and notes apps. Blocks App brings them into one organised place:
Takes get lost. Each song keeps every version side by side, with a main version you
choose.
Prompts get forgotten. The style prompt, lyrics and model that made a take stay with
the song, and prompts that worked go into a reusable catalog.
Songs aren't release-ready. Polish levels and cleans a song for streaming without
learning a DAW.
Rights are easy to lose track of. A release record notes the plan, download date and
type for every song.
It imports the files you've already downloaded. It never logs in to, scrapes or downloads
from any music service.

main one.

Editing and mixing
Editor: trim the start and end, cut out sections, fade in and out, and join another take

Release tools
A live checklist for each album: audio, titles, artist, a 3000 × 3000 cover (made in one

Export release package: every track numbered in order in distributor format, plus the

The band, live
Watch each instrument while the song plays: the drum kit lights up on every kick,
snare and hi-hat with the beat of the bar, the bass note shows as it's played, the chord
name appears with a keyboard lighting up the notes, and the singer's note shows on a
tuner.
Little note strips scroll past, including the notes coming up next.
With Suno stems, every instrument is read from its own stem, so the read-outs are
spot on. Without stems, the app reads the full mix for a good estimate.
Export what it hears as a MIDI sketch, or as a chord chart.
Vocals down: one button pulls the lead vocal out of any song so you can sing along,
and songs with stems can become a clean instrumental version.

Know your song

What makes it different
Most Suno tools are browser extensions or downloaders. Blocks App is the place your
songs live after they're made, with studio tools around them.
Built around takes. Versions are first-class: compare them by ear at the same
moment, by loudness, and by tone on one graph.
Mastering without a DAW. One click gets a song streaming-ready, and the original is
always kept.
Real measurement. Loudness uses the same standard streaming services use, so the
numbers mean something.

Privacy, speed and requirements
Private by design. Songs, prompts and settings are stored on your computer only.
Nothing is uploaded, and the app never contacts a music service.
Fast and light. It's a native Windows app (Tauri + Svelte), not a browser in disguise. It
opens in moments, stays smooth at 60 frames a second with thousands of songs, and
visuals run only while music plays.
Safe with your files. Imported audio is copied into the app's library by default, so
moving the originals breaks nothing. You can link instead. Deleted songs go to the
app's own trash first, and every change can be undone.
Requirements: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). The Windows 11 glass look needs Windows
11.
Not legal advice. The release checklist helps you keep records. Suno's terms decide
what you may do with a song.
Your first five minutes
1. Open the app. The Studio workspace is ready, with a Library, Visualizer, Lyrics pad,
Prompt catalog and Cheat sheet.
2. Drag a few of your downloaded songs onto the Library. Covers and details fill in, then
loudness, tempo and key are measured.
3. Double-click a song to play it, and watch the Visualizer. Press Ctrl+J for Now Playing.
4. Drop another take onto the same song, then switch between them with Alt+1 and
Alt+2 while it plays.
5. Right-click a song and choose Polish… › Streaming ready. Compare with A and B, then
save the new version.
6. Press Ctrl+Shift+F for the Stage view when you want a visual for a promo clip.
Everything is also in the Music menu (Alt+M) and the command palette (Ctrl+K).
